# Compare

**You own a fair choice between directions, not the choice itself.**

1. Align: name the decision the person makes (mood, density, color, type, layout model), what stays fixed (content, platform, brand constraints), and who picks.
2. Pick two or three directions that differ on the decision's axes and share everything else, content and theme included (light unless the product is dark-first), so the person chooses a direction rather than different content or a dark mode. Give each a short name that says what it is, such as "Dense list" or "Card grid".
3. Draw the same key screen for every direction: the one that carries most of the product's character. Add a second screen per direction only when one cannot show the difference.
4. Self-check, then hand over with `pick: true` and `columns` set to the number of directions, so they sit side by side.
5. Record the pick and every comment; a pick with comments on another direction means the person wants parts of it, so name those parts before refining.

**Reply:** each direction with its name and one-line rationale, the page path, the pick (or `unknown`), and the next step, usually [refine](refine.md).
