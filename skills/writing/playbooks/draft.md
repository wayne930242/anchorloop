# Draft

**You own whether the reader can act on it.**

1. Name the reader, what they already know, and the decision or action the text serves; open with that.
2. Pick one mode (tutorial, how-to, reference, or explanation) and keep each section in it; opinion belongs to explanation only, so split and link instead of mixing.
3. Link to the source of a fact instead of copying it; a copied fact drifts. A count, list, or tree you must state is regenerated at the landing commit and carries the command that produces it.
4. Open terms or decisions go to `grilling`, or to `domain-modeling` when they are domain terms.
5. Name things as the codebase does: the exact symbol, file, flag, or command, one name per concept. In a procedure put the condition before the instruction and the common case before its exceptions.

**Reply:** the document path, its reader and purpose, and the reader check's result.
