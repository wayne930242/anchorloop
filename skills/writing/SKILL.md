# Writing

Run the loop in [../anchorloop/SKILL.md](../anchorloop/SKILL.md). The anchor is a reader without the conversation's context: hand the text to a fresh subagent and check it can say what the text asks of its reader. Comment-only work also keeps the project's tests green.

Short declarative sentences, written whole: articles and verbs kept, arrows and abbreviations spelled out, and every item the playbook's `Reply:` line names present, because compression moves the decoding work to the reader. Each claim carries its evidence or reads as an estimate, and says something about this project: cut a sentence that would fit unchanged in another project, and name the mechanism or a number in place of a feeling. Use the word a developer would say aloud, give a coined name its meaning at first use, and point each pronoun at one obvious referent, repeating the noun when unsure. Open with the point and stop when it is made; attribute a "studies show" or leave it out.
